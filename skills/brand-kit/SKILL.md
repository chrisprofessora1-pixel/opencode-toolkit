# Brand Kit

Apply this kit to any content or design you create for Chris Castelli (@profchriscastelli). Everything should look and sound like her: warm, sharp, direct, no hype.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Warm black | `#1C1610` | Main text, dark backgrounds |
| Warm beige | `#FEFCF8` | Main background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, clean surfaces |
| Beige alt | `#F2E8D8` | Secondary blocks, chips |
| Espresso | `#4C3D37` | CTAs, highlights, active states |
| Chá rosa | `#D8A490` | Moment accents: badges, italic words, on dark boxes |
| Berry | `#A65A66` | Energy: launches, moments of courage |

Rules:
- Never use burgundy, gold, cold grays or blues.
- Max one or two moment colors per piece.
- Rose on warm black is the signature pair.
- On dark backgrounds use cream or white, never espresso.
- Muted text: warm brown at about 70% of espresso.

## Fonts

- Display: Sora, weights 700 and 800, for headings.
- Body: DM Sans, for running text and UI.
- Editorial: Lora italic, for single accent words and pull quotes.

## Voice

Warm and sharp, direct, spoken, empathetic, no marketing-speak.

- Lift the reader before you correct anything.
- Start with an opinion, never with a warm-up.
- Use the contrast move "it's not X, it's Y" once or twice, not more.
- One language per piece.
- No em dashes.
- No fake suspense questions.
- Show scenes instead of stacking adjectives.
- Jokes in parentheses, when they earn their place.
- Forbidden: "leverage", "deliver value", "unlock", "game changer", hype.
- Make a pile, then land a short flat line.

## Sample lines

- "Copy, paste, make them yours."
- "This is what I'm using now."
- "Your class already has a signature. You just don't know where it lives."
- "Belief without behavior is a mood, not a method."
